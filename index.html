01

About

I'm a data scientist working in clinical research. Since November 2024 I've been at the Istituto di Ricerche Farmacologiche Mario Negri in Ranica, Italy, where my work is intensive-care data: harmonising fragmented clinical databases, building the analytical datasets that other people's studies run on, and doing the statistical work that goes with them.

Two things matter to me in this work. The first is that the people who need the answers can get at them without going through me. I've built a dashboard with an embedded language-model assistant so researchers can query study data in plain language, and a no-code extraction workbook that let a physician analyse ICU data herself for her residency thesis. The second is being honest about what the data can and cannot support. That's most of what working alongside clinicians and statisticians has taught me.

Before Mario Negri I spent six months in the UK at The Openwork Partnership, building anomaly detection for regulated financial advisory data, and earlier I was a teaching assistant in machine learning at the University of Bergamo. I'm Persian, and Bergamo is home.
